HybridHELIX Consulting uses this page to identify its official public accounts, note any reserved but inactive handles, and provide public statements about platforms where we have chosen not to participate.

A social profile, handle, or community should be treated as officially ours only if it is listed on this page or linked from another page on `hybridhelix.consulting`. In some cases we reserve a name without actively using the platform. A name or profile existing under our brand does **not** by itself mean that the platform is active for us.

If you believe an account is impersonating HybridHELIX, please report it through our [contact form](https://hybridhelix.consulting/#contact) or by email at [info@hybridhelix.consulting](mailto:info@hybridhelix.consulting).

## Reddit
{: #reddit}

**Status.** Reserved name only. HybridHELIX does not maintain an active presence on Reddit.

**Statement.** At HybridHELIX, supporting small businesses and protecting user privacy sit at the center of how we operate. Reddit's API changes shut down the independent developers who had built better, more privacy-respecting ways to use the platform. In our view, those changes narrowed user choice and widened the door to data exploitation. Both run against what we stand for.

So we don't participate there. We do not log in, post, or reply on Reddit, and no one posting there is speaking for HybridHELIX.

We've given real thought to establishing a HybridHELIX presence on Mastodon, and we may revisit that. But a public community is a commitment, not a landing page. It needs consistent moderation, clear standards, and people with the time to hold both. We aren't resourced for that today, and we would rather wait than open a space we can't look after properly.

If that changes, we'll say so here first.

Mastodon remains a platform worth exploring on your own: decentralized, open-source, and built to give people more control over their experience.

If our position changes, we will update this page.
