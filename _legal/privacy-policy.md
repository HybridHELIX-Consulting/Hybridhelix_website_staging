**HybridHELIX Consulting LLC** ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website and the individuals who use our services ("Services"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your Personal Information and other data.

By accessing our website or using our Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information, including:

### Personal Information You Provide Directly

- **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number, company name, job title when you fill out contact forms, request information, or register for an account.
- **Account/Dashboard Information:** When you create or use a client account, we may collect and store information related to your projects, invoices, payment history (excluding full payment card details), account balances, communication preferences, and any other information you store within your dashboard.
- **Intake Form Information:** As part of our client onboarding or application process, we collect detailed business and personal information through forms like our "Pre-Discovery Form." This may include, but is not limited to: company structure, financial details (revenue streams, expense breakdowns, potentially financial statements), capitalization tables, operational infrastructure (tech stack, supply chain), HR information (org charts, leadership profiles, turnover rates), strategic objectives, challenges, risk assessments, company culture details, and other sensitive business data necessary to evaluate and provide our bespoke services.
- **Payment Information:** While we utilize third-party payment processors (like Stripe and PayPal) to handle transactions, we may collect information necessary to initiate or manage payments, such as billing address or bank details for wire transfers (which you initiate through your bank). We do not directly store full credit card numbers. Information provided to third-party processors is subject to their respective privacy policies.
- **Marketing Information:** Email address and preferences when you subscribe to newsletters or marketing communications.
- **Support Information:** Information you provide when contacting us for support or inquiries, including the content of your communications.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Log Data:** When you visit our website, our servers automatically record information ("Log Data"), including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and clickstream data.
- **Cookies and Tracking Technologies:** We use cookies (small text files stored on your device) and similar technologies (like web beacons, pixels) for various purposes:
  - **Essential/Functional Cookies:** Necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., user login sessions, dashboard functionality).
  - **Analytics Cookies:** To understand how visitors interact with our website, track traffic sources, and improve user experience (e.g., Google Analytics).
  - **Advertising Cookies:** To deliver relevant advertising, track ad performance, and potentially build a profile of your interests (used for our own marketing efforts).
- **Session Recording:** We may use tools like Microsoft Clarity to record user sessions, capturing mouse movements, clicks, scrolls, and other interactions on our website to understand user behavior and improve usability. This data is typically anonymized or pseudonymized where possible.
- **Device Information:** Information about the device you use to access our website, such as device type, model, and unique identifiers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:

- **Providing and Managing Services:** To set up and maintain your account, deliver the requested Services (including the Momentum Accelerator, Growth Catalyst, or Strategic COO & Operations Command), process payments, manage projects, and facilitate communication.
- **Client Assessment and Onboarding:** To review intake form information, assess suitability for our bespoke services, determine service requirements, and manage the client engagement process.
- **Communication:** To respond to your inquiries, send service-related updates, provide support, and send administrative information.
- **Website Improvement:** To analyze website usage, monitor performance, understand user behavior (including through session recordings), diagnose technical issues, and improve website functionality and user experience.
- **Marketing and Advertising:** To send newsletters, promotional materials, and other marketing communications you have opted into. We may also use analytics and advertising cookies to inform our own marketing strategies and measure campaign effectiveness.
- **Personalization:** To tailor website content or service offerings based on your preferences or interactions.
- **Security:** To protect the security and integrity of our website, services, and user data; prevent fraud; and enforce our terms.
- **Legal Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or governmental requests.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your Personal Information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers and Partners:** We share information with trusted third-party companies and individuals who perform services on our behalf ("Data Processors"). This includes payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), website hosting providers, analytics providers (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity), CRM systems, email marketing platforms, cloud storage providers, and potentially other specialized software providers necessary to deliver our Services or manage our operations. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership or uses of your Personal Information.
- **With Your Consent:** We may share your information with other third parties when we have your explicit consent to do so.

## 4. Cookies and Your Choices

Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. You can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your ability to use certain features, such as logging into your client dashboard.

You can often manage your preferences for analytics and advertising cookies through cookie consent banners or settings provided on our website, or through the settings of the third-party providers themselves (e.g., Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on).

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your Personal Information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction. We treat information submitted via intake forms with the highest standards of confidentiality, limiting internal access. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your Personal Information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (e.g., for tax, accounting, or legal requirements). We retain client dashboard and project information for the duration of the client relationship and for a reasonable period thereafter as needed for record-keeping or potential future engagements. Data collected for analytics or session recording is typically retained for a shorter period necessary for analysis.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your Personal Information:

- **Access:** You may have the right to request access to the Personal Information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** You may have the right to request correction of inaccurate Personal Information.
- **Deletion:** You may have the right to request deletion of your Personal Information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal obligations, ongoing service provision).
- **Opt-Out of Marketing:** You can opt-out of receiving promotional emails from us by following the unsubscribe instructions included in those emails or by contacting us directly. You may still receive essential service-related communications.
- **Cookie Preferences:** You can manage cookie preferences as described in Section 4.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

## 8. Children's Privacy

## 9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites not operated or controlled by us. This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our website or Services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
