## 1. Services

Service Provider will provide personalized productivity support services as part of the Momentum Accelerator plan ("Services"). These Services are designed for busy entrepreneurs, solopreneurs, investors, and small teams seeking structure, accountability, and expert guidance to enhance productivity and achieve goals faster.

**Services include:**

- **Weekly Strategy Sessions:** One (1) 1-hour session per week to review goals, identify obstacles, create action plans, and maintain focus.
- **Project Buildout Assistance:** Expert guidance on structuring projects, breaking them down into manageable tasks, and setting realistic timelines.
- **Accountability Tracking:** Regular check-ins and progress monitoring to stay motivated and on course.
- **Strategic Gap Analysis:** Leveraging expertise to identify blind spots in thinking or execution and uncover creative solutions.
- **Optional "Get It Done" Work Sessions:** Dedicating a portion of the weekly session to focused work time on specific tasks, providing hands-on support and accountability.

Any amendments or modifications to the scope of Services require a written change control document signed by both parties.

## 10. Term, Termination, Session Rollover, and Refunds

**Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues based on the selected payment plan (month-to-month, bi-weekly, or annually) until terminated by either party as outlined below.

**Session/Hour Rollover:** Unused allocated hours from a billing period automatically roll over and are added to the Client's available hour balance for subsequent periods. These rolled-over hours do not expire and can be used for sessions or other support as agreed. Client must have an active (paid) plan to utilize rolled-over hours.

**Client Cancellation & Refunds:**

- **Monthly Payments:** Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice. Termination is effective immediately. Refunds for the current billing month will be prorated based on unused weekly session hours for that specific month. Client will be refunded for any full weekly 1-hour session slots remaining in the billing month that have not yet occurred or been scheduled. Rolled-over hours from previous months are non-refundable. Furthermore, any weekly session hours from the current month that are unused but are more than 45 days past their original allocation date are also non-refundable. The value of each refundable weekly session hour is calculated as the total monthly fee ($850) divided by the typical number of sessions in that month (usually 4, sometimes 5 depending on the calendar).
- **Bi-Weekly Payments:** Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice. Client forfeits the service cost ($450) for the current bi-weekly period upon cancellation, regardless of session usage or rolled-over hours. **Exception:** If Client cancels within the first three (3) calendar days of making the bi-weekly payment AND the first session of that bi-weekly period has not occurred, Client may cancel with no further obligation for that period and receive a full refund for that $450 payment. Rolled-over hours are non-refundable under the bi-weekly plan.
- **Annual Payments:** Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice. The following cancellation guidelines apply:
  - **Cancellation within 90 Days:** If Client cancels within the first ninety (90) calendar days of the annual term start date, Client may be eligible for a prorated refund. The refund amount will be calculated as follows: The initial annual payment ($8,000) minus the non-refundable annual discount amount (calculated as [$850 x 12] - $8,000 = $2,200), minus the value of any weekly sessions used or scheduled during the first 90 days (calculated at the standard monthly prorated rate per session). The non-refundable discount amount ($2,200) is forfeited under all circumstances upon cancellation.
  - **Cancellation after 90 Days:** If Client cancels after the first ninety (90) calendar days of the annual term, the annual fee ($8,000) is non-refundable. No refunds, full or partial, will be issued, regardless of session usage or rolled-over hours.

**Service Provider Termination:** Service Provider may terminate this Agreement for any reason with three (3) days' written notice, or immediately without prior notice in the event of a material breach by Client (including non-payment). Upon termination by Service Provider, refunds, if applicable, will be processed according to the rules outlined above for the Client's specific payment plan.

**Outstanding Payments:** Upon termination by either party for any reason, Service Provider will invoice Client for any outstanding payments due up to the effective date of termination, which are due immediately upon receipt.
